Design Thinking

An Introduction to the Course and its Rationale.

What is Design Thinking?

At its core, Design Thinking is simply a process of making a series of informed decisions to improve any situation.

Why This Course?

We believe that empathy, iterative learning, and problem-solving are fundamental human traits, not exclusive talents reserved for "creatives".

  • Demystify Design Thinking: Strip away academic jargon as much as possible and understand the rest better in terms of simple words.
  • Biological Instinct: Treat it as a natural human capability.
  • Real-world Application: Harness it deliberately to solve complex problems.

🗣️ A quick note on Jargon

Throughout this course, you'll encounter some corporate words like Stakeholders, Viability, and Divergent Thinking. Don't let them intimidate you! A little bit of jargon is important to learn so that you don't get fooled by the corporate world. We'll explain what these terms actually mean as we go.
