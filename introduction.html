Module 02: Genesis

The Human Genesis

How we transformed survival into the choreography of design.

The Architecture of Decision Making

Design thinking isn't a modern invention; it's the formalization of how humans have always solved problems. To understand the frameworks we use today, we must first look at the primal scenarios of friction and empathy. The following interactive games simulate fundamental human interactions that act as the genesis of all design logic. Play through them to see how natural instincts align with professional design strategies.

01: Johnny's Dilemma

Johnny's Dilemma

Context & Complexity: Based on the classic nursery rhyme 'Johny Johny Yes Papa'. This isn't just a story about a boy and sugar; it's a simulation of Clarity, Ideation, and Execution. In design, we often face 'Red-Handed' moments where our first prototype fails publicly. This game explores how we pivot our strategy based on friction.

Please select an option in the game below to continue.

The Sugar Crisis

You've been caught with the jar open. Your father is walking into the kitchen. The feedback is immediate and high-stakes.

What is your first "Ideation" (Prototype)?

Outcome: System Failure

The Lie Failed. Your father sees the sugar on your face. You've created more friction, and trust is broken. In design, a bad prototype leads to a 'Hard Stop'.

Outcome: Human Resonance

Transparency Succeeded. By being honest, you removed the conflict. Your father smiles and talks to you about health. You solved the problem through Empathy for his concern.

Outcome: The Lucky Pivot

You escaped for now, but the root cause (your hunger and the open jar) hasn't been addressed. This is a 'Patch'—it works once, but doesn't solve the long-term architecture.

03: Doctor's Loop

The Doctor's Loop

Context & Complexity: A doctor doesn't just guess a diagnosis; they follow a strict loop of Assess (Empathy), Prescribe (Prototyping), and Follow-up (Iteration). In this scenario, a patient walks in with a mysterious headache. How you navigate their symptoms dictates their recovery.

Please select an option in the game below to see the outcome.

The Assessment

A patient complains of severe migraines. What is your first step?

Outcome: The Shallow Fix

Prescription Failed. The painkillers masked the symptom, but the migraines returned. You failed to assess the root cause (Lack of Empathy).

The Prescription

You discover they stare at a screen for 12 hours a day with poor posture. What is your prototype solution?

Outcome: Unrealistic Prototype

System Rejected. Quitting their job isn't a viable solution for their livelihood. Your prototype didn't account for the user's constraints.

Outcome: The Healing Loop

Iteration Success. The patient returns a week later. The migraines are gone. You successfully completed the loop: Assess (Empathy) → Prescribe (Execution) → Follow-up (Iteration).
