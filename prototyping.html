Module 05 · Prototyping

The Scope of Prototyping

Prototyping is the act of making ideas tangible. The scope of prototyping is vast, spanning from digital interfaces to physical objects and electronic systems. While a few of these will be discussed in detail within our limitations, all serve the same goal: communicating intent and testing assumptions.

The Catch: Bounded Rationality & MAYA

Herbert Simon noted that we are only able to comprehend ideas within the scope of things we already believe or know—a concept called Bounded Rationality. This is both a limitation and an opportunity. It's an opportunity because as we learn more, we widen the scope of ideas we can generate. But it's a limitation because if our users don't understand our prototype, they will instantly reject it.

This is where Raymond Loewy's MAYA principle comes in: Most Advanced, Yet Acceptable. Getting to know the users deeply during the Empathy phase helps us uncover their MAYA threshold. By anchoring new, advanced features in concepts they are already familiar with, we can design prototypes that feel highly innovative, yet completely intuitive to them.
